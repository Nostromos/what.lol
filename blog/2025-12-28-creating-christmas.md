Children need ritual and routine. There's so much about life they don't know that we can sugar their perceptions with a bit of mysticism and tradition. This gives them something to look back upon fondly in the future and builds bonds/ties with family and friends, strengthening social bonds.

I want my children to look back on Christmas warmly - a time of love, comfort, and closeness with loved ones. It's been written many millions of times but there's so much anger and bad in our world that it's immensely satisfying to create a happy, memorable experience for a small child, especially so when they're deeply loved.

<!--truncate-->

I think back to my own Christmas memories and the first thing that comes to mind is presents under a decorated Christmas tree. We recently had a second child, a son, and at the same time, our daughter is almost two. It's been a big transition for her lately so I wanted to give her a great experience, with her centered (and a bit spoiled). It's the first Christmas she can be truly present and conscious of what is going on (or mostly conscious).
